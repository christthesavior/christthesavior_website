Who We Are

A welcoming church family, gathered from many nations

Christ the Savior Church is home to believers, seekers, and newcomers to Podgorica alike. We are a Protestant, non‑denominational congregation shaped by Scripture, prayer, and genuine friendship — not by program or performance.

Whether you grew up in the faith or are simply curious, you are welcome exactly as you are. We keep our gatherings warm and unhurried, with room for real conversation before and after every service.

Connect with God. Engage the culture. Build real community.

Our Focus

What we give ourselves to

I.

Scripture & Discipleship

We take the Bible seriously and walk alongside one another, helping each person take the next honest step of faith.

II.

Community & Belonging

From English Club to Bible study, our week is built around real relationships — not just Sunday mornings.

III.

Serving Podgorica

We open our doors to our neighbors, partner with local ministries, and give practically where we can.
