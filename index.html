Who We Are

A welcoming church family, gathered from many nations

Christ the Savior Church is home to believers, seekers, and newcomers to Podgorica alike. We are a Protestant, non‑denominational congregation immersed in Scripture, prayer, forming a welcoming community based on true connection and friendship, not by program or performance.

Whether you grew up in the faith or are simply curious, you are welcome exactly as you are. We keep our gatherings warm and unhurried, with room for connecting before and after every service.

Connect with God. Dive into the Word. Build a real community.

Our Focus

What we give ourselves to

I.

Scripture & Discipleship

We take the Bible seriously and walk alongside one another, helping each person take the next step of faith.

II.

Community & Belonging

From English Club to Bible Study and our community center's activities, our week is built around real relationships — not just Sunday mornings.

III.

Serving Podgorica

We open our doors to our neighbors, partner with local ministries, and serve the community in whatever capacity we can.
